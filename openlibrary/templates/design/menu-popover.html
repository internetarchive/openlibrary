Menu Popover

A trigger paired with a popover menu of mutually-exclusive choices — the sort-menu shape used across the web. Items render as a plain list with the current one highlighted; there are no radio controls, because nothing is being held as a value.

Menu popover or options popover?

The two look similar and behave differently. Pick by what activating an item does:

  • <ol-options-popover> is a form control — a radiogroup that holds a value, participates in forms via name, shows its radios, and moves its selection as you arrow through it (selection follows focus). Reach for it when the value is read later, or submitted.
  • <ol-menu-popover> is an action menu — arrows move focus only, and activating an item does something and closes. Nothing is committed until Enter or a click, which is what makes it safe to wire to navigation or any other change you can't undo by arrowing back. It is deliberately not form-associated.

The sort control on search, author, list, and reading-log pages is the canonical consumer: choosing an item loads a new URL, so arrowing past "Trending" must not navigate.

Basic menu

Give it items, a value for the current choice, and a label that names the menu. Without a slotted trigger, an ol-button is injected showing the current item's label.

<ol-menu-popover
  label="Sort by"
  value="relevance"
  items='[{"value":"relevance","label":"Relevance"}, ...]'>
</ol-menu-popover>

Nested items

Mark an item nested to indent it as a subset of the item above. The reading-log sorts use this to hang shelf filters under their parent sort.

items='[
  {"value":"readinglog","label":"Reading Log"},
  {"value":"want_to_read","label":"Want to Read","nested":true},
  {"value":"already_read","label":"Already Read","nested":true}
]'

Custom trigger via slot

Slot your own trigger when you need control over its size or want it server-rendered — a server-rendered trigger has no layout shift while the component upgrades, which is why the sort control slots one instead of taking the default.

<ol-menu-popover label="Sort by" value="index" items="...">
  <ol-button slot="trigger" size="small">List Order</ol-button>
</ol-menu-popover>

Keyboard & accessibility

The panel is a menu of menuitemradio items, with the current one carrying aria-checked. Arrow keys and Home/End move focus between items, wrapping at the ends, and select nothing; Enter, Space, or a click activates the focused item and closes the menu. Escape closes without selecting, and focus returns to the trigger. A roving tabindex keeps the whole menu a single tab stop, and pressing Down on the trigger opens the menu onto the current item.
